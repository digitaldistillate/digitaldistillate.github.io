Can Haz Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Can Haz app for iPhone, made by Digital Distillate.

Camera

Can Haz uses your iPhone's camera to detect bananas and measure their distance. All processing happens on your device. Camera images and measurements are never saved, collected, or sent anywhere.

Data Stored on Your Device

Can Haz keeps a count of the bananas you've confirmed. This count is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Data We Collect

None. Can Haz does not collect any personal information, use analytics, show ads, or share data with anyone.
