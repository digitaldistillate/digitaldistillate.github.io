Can Haz

Point your iPhone camera at a banana and Can Haz tells you if it's close enough: CAN HAZ if it's within 1 meter, NO CAN HAZ if it's farther away.

Requirements

Can Haz requires an iPhone with a LiDAR Scanner to measure distance. That includes iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max, and later Pro and Pro Max models.
